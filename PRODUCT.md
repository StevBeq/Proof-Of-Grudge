# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-pinned: React 19, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, vanilla Three.js, GSAP and Lenis. Self-hosted Fontsource fonts. No browser wallet or keys.

## Users

Hackathon judges and automated evaluators learning the four-branch bond mechanism without accounts or WebGL.

## Product Purpose

Make adversarial inheritance understandable in ten seconds and let visitors explore all four outcomes in a local simulator. Inspect a configured NEAR Testnet vault without signing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Three distinct actors: Owner, Heir and Rival. Symmetric bonds. Strict deadline boundaries. Pull-payment credits are not completed transfers. ETH release is destination-locked to the heir on Sepolia, chain 11155111. Simulation must never imply a real signature or broadcast.

English defaults; Indonesian translates the interface with a persisted language preference. Reduced motion and SVG are first-class paths. No stretch AI, Bitcoin, extra heirs or tokens.

## Brand Commitments

Proof-of-Grudge. Preserve the user-authored DESIGN.md: bureaucratic voice, three actor colors, three-ring monolith, state separate from outcome.

## Evidence on Hand

Contract deployed on NEAR Testnet (`pog-mw835qrr-1c82b2-vault.testnet`), with all four native payoff branches, zero remaining FullAccess keys, NEAR MPC signing (Domain 0), and destination-locked Ethereum Sepolia release executed and verified on-chain. See `scripts/TESTNET.md` for complete transaction receipts and hashes.

## Hosting & Web Deployment

The web simulator is deployed in production at `https://proof-of-grudge.vercel.app` with Vite, React 19, and procedural Three.js cel-shaded rendering, with fallback support for reduced motion and SVG.

## Product Principles

- Explain incentives rather than make trust claims.
- Separate simulated state, observed state, credits and completed transfers.
- Keep the browser read-only and public configuration secret-free.
- Preserve useful behavior without motion, WebGL or live connectivity.
